Daily Donut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-26

Daily Donut collects, transmits, or shares no personal data. The game is played offline; the only exception is that the optional Premium purchase goes through Apple's App Store.

Data collected

None. The app contains no analytics or tracking, shows no ads, and creates no accounts. The optional Premium purchase and "restore" are handled by Apple; we never see or store your payment information.

Stored on device

Only your game progress (day streak, best score, your daily results history) and sound/haptics preferences are kept in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, or servers.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and, because it collects no data from anyone, collects no data from children.
